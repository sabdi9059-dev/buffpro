Frequently asked questions

How does payment work? +

Customers pay securely by card through Stripe when they book. Funds are deposited to your bank — typically the same or next business day. You can also collect a deposit and charge the balance later.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel from your dashboard whenever you like — your 30-day beta is completely free.

Do my customers need to download an app? +

No. They book from any browser via a link you share. Everything works great on a phone — no downloads, no accounts required to book.

Will I get reminders sent automatically? +

Yes. COATPRO texts customers a confirmation when they book and a reminder 24 hours before the appointment, which dramatically reduces no-shows.

Can I add my technicians? +

On the Business plan you can add up to 15 staff. Each technician sees only the jobs assigned to them and can mark them complete from their phone.

Is my data secure? +

Absolutely. Payments are handled by Stripe (PCI-compliant) and your business data is isolated so no one else can ever see it.
